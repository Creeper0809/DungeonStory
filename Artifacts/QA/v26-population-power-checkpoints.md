## Authority notes

- Biological births are dependents until species adulthood: myconid 180d, slime 240d, kobold 300d, orc/beastkin/harpy 420d, demon/vampire/human 540d; constructed golems are adults after assembly.
- Early working-adult growth therefore depends on adult recruitment, captivity recruitment and golem assembly rather than instant child labor.
- Combat proficiency assumes a focused resident can earn at most 2 safe-training XP/day; field, construction and food XP are conservative approved-work shares.
- Loadouts are real authored equipment definitions projected through CombatEquipmentRuntime. Only the checkpoint bands and training shares are theoretical targets.
- Required power is a readiness gate, not a win-rate proof. Multi-seed battle calibration remains required.
